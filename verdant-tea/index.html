Five steps, six minutes, no equipment worth arguing about

Good tea is mostly restraint. Use more leaf than feels right, less time than you expect, and water that has gone off the boil.

  1. 01

    Warm the vessel

    Rinse the pot and cups with hot water and tip it out. Cold porcelain steals heat from the first steep.

    30 s
  2. 02

    Measure by weight

    Five grams to 150 ml. Spoons lie — a rolled oolong and a needle white fill the same spoon at half the mass.

    5 g / 150 ml
  3. 03

    Wake the leaf

    A three second rinse for oolong and pu-erh. Discard it. Greens and whites skip this entirely.

    3 s
  4. 04

    Steep off the boil

    80 °C for green and white, 95 °C for oolong and pu-erh. Boiling water scorches delicate leaf and turns it bitter.

    80–95 °C
  5. 05

    Pour it all out

    Leave nothing in the pot. Leaf sitting in water keeps brewing, and the second cup pays for it.

    45 s
